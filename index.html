Master plan

The Alice Memorial Education & Skills Development Campus

One site where a young person can finish secondary school, learn a trade, get treated at the health centre and learn modern farming. Tap the image to see it at full size.

Concept render of the planned campus. It shows the full vision; the list below says what is already running, what is being built and what is still planned.

What the campus will include

  • Secondary school for 1,000+ students, with boys' and girls' hostels
  • Library and ICT lab, innovation and entrepreneurship centre
  • Vocational institute: carpentry and joinery, welding and metal fabrication, electrical installation, tailoring and fashion design, hairdressing and cosmetology, ICT and digital skills
  • HCIV health centre with outpatient, maternity, general, children's and isolation wards, theatre and labour room, pharmacy, X-ray and dental unit
  • Sports complex, administration block, large green areas and solar energy

What it should change

  • Quality education for 1,000+ students
  • Skills training for 500+ trainees every year
  • Quality healthcare close to the community
  • Jobs and opportunity for young people
  • Food security and income from farming

Alice Memorial College

A secondary school for Buikwe

Alice Memorial College will serve more than 1,000 students, both day scholars and boarders, from S.1 to S.6. Places will be subsidised for families from Buikwe District, so a good secondary school is within reach of the children who live around it.

The campus is designed to stay green, with large open lawns and tree-lined walkways kept between the buildings, and with solar energy to power it. We expect to start by 2028.

The college is foundation-aided. Government will be involved where possible, and we are open to any partnership.

Alice Sports Academy

Where local talent gets trained

Football for boys and netball for girls are running today. Each side has coaches who train the players regularly, to build talent and keep it. Our under-12 and under-14 teams have played at national level, where players received medals for taking part, and have won trophies closer to home.

Progress so far

What is built and what is next

The foundation has not waited for the full campus. Several parts are already working, and each has a clear next step.

  • Primary school

    Running

    Classes continue and the school is growing. We are in talks with World Vision about sponsorships so learners from less privileged homes can study beyond primary.

  • Sports academy

    Running

    Football for boys and netball for girls, with coaches who train both sides regularly. The under-12 and under-14 teams have won trophies at district, zonal and regional level and have played at national level. More below.

  • Health facility

    Building up

    A doctor, a nurse and a midwife are on staff. The facility has beds with mattresses, testing kits and basic drugs, and an ultrasound scan is on the way. The next step is constructing the permanent HCIV building.

  • Vocational school

    Building up

    Registered. We are deciding which workshop equipment to buy first.

  • Alice Agro-farms

    Building up

    Cocoa, coffee, banana, horticulture, livestock, poultry and mechanised farming are under way, with veterinary and agriculture staff in place. It opens to students and farmers in 2027. More below.

  • Alice Memorial College

    Planned

    A foundation-aided secondary school for 1,000+ day and boarding students, S.1 to S.6, with subsidised places for Buikwe residents. Government will be involved where possible, and we are open to any partnership. Campus plans, crest and uniforms are designed. We expect to start by 2028.

  • Electricity

    Planned

    A 50 kVA transformer and an overhead line to the primary school are being planned. Government has indicated that grid power will reach the area in 2027.

  • Community programs

    Planned

    Programs for adults and older age groups, including talks with local councils about extending the sports academy beyond youth teams.

Other programs

Work already delivered

Beyond the hub, the foundation has carried out other programs for the community. They include agricultural mechanisation, a dental health outreach, a priest's house and a community church. A medical outreach is now in progress.

Alice Agro-farms

A working farm that teaches

Alice Agro-farms shows students, neighbours and visitors what to grow, how to look after animals and when to plant. We have employed veterinary personnel and agriculturalists to run the farm and to test and master every practice first. Only then will we open the farm to the public, in 2027.

We have bought an irrigation pump and dug a deep water well. Learners and visitors will see good practice across every crop and animal we keep.
